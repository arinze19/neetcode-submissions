PART 1
CARL method [3 mins]
C - Context [10%]
A - Action [60%]
R - Results [20%]
L - Learning [10%]

--------------------------------------
Behavioural code: Decode, Select, Deliver
--------------------------------------

Core Signal Areas:
1. Scope 
2. Ownership 
3. Ambiguity 
4. Perseverance 
5. Conflict resolution 
6. Growth 
7. Communication 
8. Leadership 

STAR method
1. Situation: Set Context    
    - describe where working, team and nature of project
    - focus on details that will matter later in the story 

2. Task: Define your responsibility
    - Outline the problem YOU were responsible for 
    - include relevant deadlines or constraints

3. Action: Explain what you did 
    - Explain your thought process 
    - highlight techincal, anaytical and interpersonal skills where relevant

4. Result: Share the outcome 
    - Highlight process improvements or lessons learned 
    - Use numbers or metrics when possible
