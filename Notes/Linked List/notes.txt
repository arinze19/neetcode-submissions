    - 1st write: unlink (pointing previous node to None)
    - 2nd write: attach to new position (pointing node.next to its new next)
    - 3rd write: rewriting the head/tail/prev.next variable
3. Principle for getting the order right in linked list operations 
    - wire up your new connections before you tear down old ones
5. Remember the checklist when handling operations in linked list

    - empty 
    - first 
    - last

7. linked lists store a lot for the object overhead (bytes for the data + bytes for the information to the next data et al)
