=================== GREEDY PROPERTY
Keyword: minimum, fewest, longest

1. Greedy choice property: local optimal choice leads to global optimal choice 
2. Optimal substructure: solving smaller subproblems solve whole problem 
3. No backtracking: decision is final

Usually deals with sorting and co

Greedy Decision Framework

Step 1: Can I sort the input to make greedy work?
Step 2: Is there a clear "best local choice" at each step?
Step 3: Can I find a counterexample where greedy fails?
        YES → need DP or backtracking
        NO  → greedy likely works!
Step 4: Prove greedy works via exchange argument:
        "Swapping any greedy choice for a non-greedy choice
         never improves the solution"
