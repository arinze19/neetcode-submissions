7. in a directed list the entry count halves?? -> entries same as edges 
8. adjacency matrix used for representing compact graphs
    -> An undirected graph gives a symmetric matrix. A weighted graph stores the weight in place of 1 and uses 0, None or infinity to mean "no edge"
9.  - sparse matrix -> E ~ V 
    - dense matrix -> E ~ V^2
10. implicit graph 
    -> neighbors/edges computed on demand 
    -> each coordinate is a vertex
    -> cells that share a side are adjacent
11. directed v undirected 
    -> reachability is not symmetric in a directed graph
12. weighted v unweighted graphs 
13. runtime 
    - every node visited at most once -> O(V)
    - every edge inspected at most twice -> O(E) # loop for neighbor in adj_list[node]
    - total: O(V + E)
