=================== PATTERNS ===================
1. circular queue 
2. streaming pattern (data expires as it moves out of stream scope)
    - sliding/count based window (fixed) -> addition of an item at the back constitutes removal of another item at the front
    - streaming/time based window (unfixed) -> addition of an item at the front can constitute removal of several items at the front
3. BFS on grids, graphs and trees 
4. topological sort
5. monotonic deque
